# Code of Conduct

MOECoG follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, in full. The short version:

- Be respectful and welcoming. Disagreement about methods is normal; disrespect is not.
- Give credit: datasets, pipelines and results carry the names and citations of the people who produced them.
- Respect data-use terms. Do not ask for, share, or commit data or credentials that a licence or agreement forbids.
- Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at yifanyu97@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately and
  promptly; the maintainers may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban participants who breach these standards.

This applies to every project space: the repository, issues and pull requests, documentation, and any discussion
channel the project adopts.
