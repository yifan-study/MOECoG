    What MOABB did, and what MOECoG copies¶

    MOABB (Mother of all BCI Benchmarks, NeuroTechX) is the model for this project. Facts below were read from the repository on 2026-09-10 (fork at yifan-study/moabb, upstream NeuroTechX/moabb) and from GitHub.

    What we copy¶

    1. Package first. Versioned releases on PyPI, CHANGELOG.md, CITATION.cff, CI on every PR. A release is a promise that the loaders in it work.
    2. Datasets and pipelines as small PRs. A loader plus a catalog entry plus a smoke-test record is one PR. A pipeline is one YAML file with a citation.
    3. Results live in the repo and are regenerated by scripts. Nothing hand-edited. The leaderboard and the smoke table are build artefacts.
    4. A dataset summary that is computed, not written. Our docs/decodable_datasets.md already is.
    5. A one-call benchmark so a new pipeline can be scored on the reference datasets in one line.
    6. "Bring your own BIDS." BIDSiEEGDataset(root=...) already plays the role of LocalBIDSDataset.

    What we do differently, and why¶

    • Patient-specific evaluation. EEG benchmarks pool subjects; ECoG grids differ per patient, so MOECoG never stacks subjects and reports per-patient distributions (WithinSubjectCV, chronological folds, kappa headline).
    • Regression is first-class. Finger flexion, cursor, force and audio spectrogram targets sit next to trial classification, because that is where our decoders (PACE, TRACE) live.
    • A catalog with a status, not a wish list. Every dataset entry says whether it loads today and why not otherwise; blocked and unsupported are statuses, not silence.
    • Smaller surface. MOABB has 23 pipelines and dozens of datasets after nine years. MOECoG starts with the Miller library, the BCI competitions and a handful of loaders, and grows one chunk at a time (see ROADMAP.md).
    • No CodeCarbon, optuna or pseudo-online machinery yet. They can come when someone needs them.

    What "hosted on the cloud" meant for MOABB¶

    Nothing exotic: source on GitHub, package on PyPI, docs on GitHub Pages under a NeuroTechX domain, a Zenodo DOI minted from GitHub releases, and result tables committed as CSV/JSON in the repository. Data are never hosted by MOABB; loaders download from the original repositories into ~/mne_data. MOECoG follows the same split: code and numbers in the repo, data at the source, large caches on Athene or a personal disk (MOECOG_DATA_DIR).
